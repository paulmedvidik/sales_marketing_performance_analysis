# Sales & Marketing Performance Analysis

##Overview
This project is a business analytics case study to evaluate marketing effectiveness, discount incentives, forecasting sales performance, and identifying opportunities to improve profitability.

Using statistical analysis, predictive modeling, and ooptimization techniques, this project analyzes the relationship between advertising spend, discount strategies, and overall sales performance. The analysis was conducted using Python, Excel, and business intelligence techniques to provide actionable recommendations for executive planning.

---

##Business Problem
TechGear, Inc. needed to better understand:
- Which advertising platform generated the strongest ROI
- Whether discount strategies positively impacted sales
- How accurately future sales performance could be forecasted
- If current sales trends supported sustainability and profitability goals
- How to optimize marketing budget allocation for maximum revenue generation

The objective of this analysis was to transform raw sales and marketing data into strategic business insights that could improve financial performance and support data-driven decision making.

## Key Findings

### Marketing ROI
- Instagram advertising generated the strongest modeled ROI across analyzed variables
- Facebook advertising demonstrated a strong relationship with sales performance, but Instagram produced higher efficiency relative to spend
- Discount strategies showed diminishing returns and negatively impacted profitability

### Forecasting & Predictive Analysis
- Regression models demonstrated stronger predictive accuracy than decision tree models
- Predictive modeling indicated a risk of missing monthly profitability targets
- Exponential smoothing provided stronger short-term forecasting reliability than moving averages

### Monte Carlo Simulation
- Simulated monthly revenue averaged approximately $4,975
- Standard deviation remained relatively stable, suggesting predictable revenue variability
- Forecasted performance remained below the company profitability target of $6,500 per month

### Optimization Strategy
- Marketing budget optimization models supported increasing Instagram advertising allocation
- Analytical modeling suggested revenue growth could occur while simultaneously reducing overall advertising inefficiencies

---

## Strategic Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

- Increase Instagram advertising allocation
- Reduce dependency on discounting strategies
- Utilize short-term exponential smoothing forecasting methods
- Monitor profitability through predictive KPI dashboards
- Continuously evaluate marketing ROI through regression-based performance analysis
